Infosys adds 4,480 employees in Q2FY27, freshers hires exceed 8,000

Freshers hiring tops 8,000 as attrition falls to 12.7%

What happened

Infosys increased its headcount by 4,480 people in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027. The company hired more than 8,000 fresh graduates and its voluntary attrition rate fell to 12.7% on a last‑twelve‑month basis, down from 13% in the prior quarter.

Karnataka flags ethanol levels reaching 31% in petrol samples

Karnataka officials found petrol samples containing up to 31.2 percent ethanol content.

  • Karnataka reported petrol samples containing up to 31.2% ethanol, exceeding E20 limits.
  • Excess ethanol reduces vehicle mileage and raises concerns over OMC blending compliance.
  • Joint sampling results and official responses from oil marketing companies.
Energy & UtilitiesAutomotive

What happened

On November 6, 2026, Karnataka's Department of Food reported that petrol samples from multiple districts contained ethanol levels of up to 31.2%, exceeding the national 20% (E20) limit. The Union Oil Ministry refuted the claims on November 7, 2026, stating that 893 tested OMC petrol samples met quality standards. The ministry added that Karnataka officials failed to follow standard sampling procedures during their testing.

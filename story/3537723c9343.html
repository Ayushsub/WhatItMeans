Fairfax signs $5.6 billion deal to acquire controlling stake in IDBI Bank

Fairfax acquires a 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank from the Indian government and LIC.

  • Fairfax is acquiring 60.72% of IDBI Bank for ₹53,000 crore from government and LIC.
  • It marks the largest foreign direct investment deal in India's banking sector history.
  • Regulatory approvals and strategic execution under former HDFC executives will be watched closely.
Banking & Financials

What happened

Fairfax Financial Holdings agreed to acquire a 60.72% controlling stake in IDBI Bank for approximately ₹53,000 crore ($5.6 billion). The shares are being purchased from the Government of India and Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC). The deal was executed without primary investment bankers, and Fairfax plans to retain current MD and CEO Rakesh Sharma while engaging former HDFC leaders Deepak Parekh and Paresh Sukthankar as advisors.

The acquisition transfers majority equity control of IDBI Bank from state-backed entities to Fairfax Financial. Following required regulatory clearances over coming months, operational decision-making shifts to the new controlling shareholder. The buyer intends to preserve existing leadership under CEO Rakesh Sharma while leveraging seasoned advisors Deepak Parekh and Paresh Sukthankar to refine governance. Over subsequent quarters, this structure is expected to influence credit growth strategies, risk management, and capital deployment.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. US employment data and election results signal economic health and political policy direction
  2. Federal Reserve adjusts interest rate path expectations based on labor market tightness
  3. Changes in US bond yields shift relative returns between US dollar assets and emerging markets

US employment numbers and key corporate earnings offer market participants signals about economic expansion or cooling. Stronger labor market data typically increases expectations of sustained higher interest rates by the US Federal Reserve to keep inflation in check. Higher US interest rates increase yields on US government bonds, reducing the relative attractiveness of riskier emerging market assets like Indian stocks. Over a span of several weeks, this shift forces foreign portfolio reallocations, influencing domestic liquidity and equity valuations.

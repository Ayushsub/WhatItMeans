SEBI bars EKI Energy MD Manish Dabkara for 12 months over ₹197 crore revenue overstatement

EKI Energy’s reported ₹197.04 crore revenue now under regulator scrutiny

  • SEBI suspended the MD for 12 months over accounting misstatements

What happened

On 31 October 2026 SEBI announced a 12‑month bar on Manish Dabkara, managing director of EKI Energy Services, citing premature revenue recognition of ₹197.04 crore and fictitious expenses of ₹26.34 crore in the company’s accounts. The regulator said the misstatements violated securities law and required corrective action.

What happened

On 9 October 2026, the Reserve Bank of India issued two circulars—A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 and Circular No. 26—announcing regulatory measures for the foreign exchange market. The central bank stated that these measures were introduced in response to evolving market conditions to ensure orderly functioning.

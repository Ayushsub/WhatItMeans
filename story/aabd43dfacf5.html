The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. US inflation data is released
  2. Federal Reserve adjusts its interest rate outlook
  3. Global liquidity and investor risk appetite shift
  4. Capital flows into or out of speculative assets like Bitcoin

When US inflation data is published, it immediately signals whether the Federal Reserve can cut interest rates or must keep them higher for longer. This policy outlook alters global liquidity; higher rates make safe US Treasury bonds attractive, drawing capital away from risky assets. Conversely, lower rate expectations increase investor appetite for speculative assets like cryptocurrencies. This transmission typically reflects in crypto prices within hours of the data release, though institutional capital reallocation can take weeks to fully play out.

Bitcoin hovered near $79,000, while Ethereum and other major altcoins declined.

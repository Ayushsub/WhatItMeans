Airtel targets Rs 300 ARPU as postpaid share to rise to 20%

Postpaid share aims to jump from 8% to 20%

  • Airtel says ARPU will soon exceed Rs 300
  • Higher ARPU could lift telecom earnings
  • Watch postpaid conversion and tariff moves
TelecomTrade & Tariffs

What happened

Chief Executive Shashwat Sharma told that Bharti Airtel expects its average revenue per user (ARPU) to cross the Rs 300 mark in the near term. The company plans to increase the proportion of postpaid customers from about 8% today to roughly 20% over the long run. Recent tariff hikes and the rollout of new plans are being used to drive this shift.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. step 1: Airtel raises tariffs and pushes new postpaid plans
  2. step 2: these actions force average revenue per user upward
  3. step 3: higher ARPU flows into stronger quarterly earnings for the company
  4. step 4: earnings growth may reflect in profit margins and cash flow

The tariff increase directly lifts the price paid per user, while encouraging customers to move to postpaid contracts adds a higher‑margin revenue stream. The combined effect raises the ARPU figure, which then shows up in the firm’s earnings statements after a typical reporting lag of one to two quarters. The earnings boost can improve profit margins and cash generation, feeding back into the company’s financial health.

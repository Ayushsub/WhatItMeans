Centre orders rice and paddy stock declaration, permits limits in five southern states

Five southern states can now set rice stock caps

What happened

On 6 November 2026 the Union government announced that traders must declare their rice and paddy inventories. It also authorised five southern states to impose quantitative stock limits to deter hoarding amid rising prices.

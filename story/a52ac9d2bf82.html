SEBI studies position limits on non‑agri contracts to improve liquidity

SEBI's review of position limits targets non‑agri contract liquidity

What happened

On October 3, 2026 SEBI announced it is actively examining position limits for non‑agricultural commodity contracts to boost market liquidity and depth. The regulator also highlighted the need to address structural friction, including GST‑related issues affecting participants who trade through exchanges. The effort is part of a broader push to improve market structure.

  3. step 3: traders must adjust position sizes and hedging strategies, affecting order flow
  4. step 4: manifests as tighter bid‑ask spreads and altered turnover in non‑agri contracts

The announcement (step 1) creates regulatory pressure to redesign limits (step 2). That forces market participants to recalibrate how much they can hold (step 3), which changes the supply‑demand balance in the order book. Over weeks to months this shows up as narrower spreads and changes in contract turnover, influencing earnings of brokerage firms and exchange operators.

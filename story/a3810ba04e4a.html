Norway begins LNG shipments to India as bilateral energy ties expand

Equinor delivers its initial liquid gas cargo to a Gujarat terminal.

  • Norway started supplying liquefied natural gas to India with a delivery in Gujarat.
  • The shipments help diversify India's energy import sources beyond traditional Middle Eastern suppliers.
  • Watch for long-term gas supply contracts and joint investments in green hydrogen projects.
Oil & GasRenewable Energy

What happened

Norway's energy firm Equinor delivered a cargo of liquefied natural gas to Gujarat in October 2026. Norwegian State Secretary Astrid Bergmal confirmed that bilateral cooperation is expanding from fossil fuel shipments into green hydrogen and renewable energy initiatives. The delivery marks the formal start of expanded energy trade between the two nations.

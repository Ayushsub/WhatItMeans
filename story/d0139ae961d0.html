Parliamentary panel to examine 46 economic subjects including crypto and AI

Forty-six economic domains face comprehensive legislative review spanning artificial intelligence and crypto.

  • Parliamentary finance committee selects 46 economic issues to review in FY27.
  • Scrutiny could shape future regulatory policy across technology and financial services.

What happened

The Parliamentary Committee on Finance has decided to examine 46 economic subjects during fiscal year 2027, according to reporting on 24 October 2026. The scope includes assessing artificial intelligence, cryptocurrency, banking, pensions, health insurance, and informal sector social security. The panel will also evaluate the performance of various central government schemes.

  4. Ministries review suggestions to decide on legislative amendments or regulatory circulars

Parliamentary standing committees evaluate policy effectiveness and industry risks through structured hearings, a process typically unfolding over multiple quarters. Their findings are compiled into reports presented to Parliament, which are advisory rather than immediately binding law. Executive ministries review these recommendations and submit action-taken reports within months. Any concrete operational changes for businesses depend on whether ministries translate those recommendations into statutory bills or regulatory circulars.

RBI bought $43.26 billion forex in August as net FDI fell to $3 billion

Gross FDI fell to $11 billion in August while foreign inflows spiked currency interventions.

  • RBI made record net forex purchases of $43.26 billion while gross FDI fell.
  • Central bank interventions absorbed heavy dollar inflows from offshore borrowings and deposit schemes.
  • Monitor upcoming monthly FDI data and RBI forward swap positions.
Banking & FinancialsIT & Computer Services

What happened

In August 2026, the Reserve Bank of India recorded net foreign exchange purchases of $43.26 billion to absorb dollar inflows from overseas borrowings and non-resident deposits. During the same month, India's gross foreign direct investment inflows declined to $11 billion from $14 billion in July. Net foreign direct investment slowed to $3 billion in August, down from $7 billion in the previous month.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. Inflows from deposit schemes and foreign debt increase dollar supply in domestic forex markets
  2. RBI buys $43.26 billion net forex to prevent rapid currency appreciation
  3. RBI executes balancing forward sales and swaps to manage domestic rupee liquidity
  4. Simultaneous deceleration in foreign direct equity reduces long-term net inflows

Substantial foreign currency entering through deposit schemes and external borrowing forces the central bank to intervene directly in foreign exchange spot markets over days and weeks. To maintain exchange rate stability, the Reserve Bank of India absorbs $43.26 billion in dollars. To prevent this intervention from flooding domestic commercial banks with excess rupee liquidity, the central bank uses currency swaps and forward contracts over months. Concurrently, a drop in net foreign direct investment to $3 billion reflects reduced long-term corporate equity capital entering sectors like finance and technology.

Jargon, in plain words

FCNR(B) scheme

Net FDI
Foreign direct capital entering a country minus the direct investments made abroad by resident entities.
Forex swaps

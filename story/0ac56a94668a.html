IGL signs pact with Northern Railway to supply PNG across 22 sites

Displacing 2,500 litres of daily diesel sets up steady institutional gas demand.

  • IGL partnered with Northern Railway to supply piped natural gas across 22 Delhi-NCR facilities.
  • Secures 3,000 SCMD of commercial gas volume while cutting railway fuel emissions and costs.
  • Expansion of commercial gas contracts across other Indian Railways divisions and transit hubs.
City Gas DistributionRailwaysCrude & Energy

What happened

On October 31, 2026, Indraprastha Gas Limited signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Northern Railway's Delhi Division. Under the agreement, IGL will supply approximately 3,000 SCMD of piped natural gas to 22 railway establishments, including major stations such as New Delhi and Anand Vihar. The gas will displace about 500 kg of LPG and 2,500 litres of diesel consumed daily in base kitchens, laundries, and rest houses.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. MoU executed to supply 3,000 SCMD of PNG across 22 designated railway sites
  2. Railway facilities swap diesel generators, boilers, and kitchen LPG burners for piped gas connections
  3. Displaced liquid fuel purchases lower Northern Railway's utility operating costs
  4. IGL records stable, non-vehicular commercial gas sales with minimal credit risk

The partnership initiates infrastructure build-out to connect gas lines directly to railway kitchens, laundries, and rest facilities over coming months. As each site connects, it eliminates the need to transport and store 2,500 litres of diesel and 500 kg of LPG daily, lowering utility overhead within 1-2 quarters. For IGL, locking in public-sector anchor clients builds high-retention commercial demand, showing up as recurring quarterly distribution volumes. Over longer periods, this model creates a template for converting other railway divisions and government campuses.

Jargon, in plain words

SCMD
Standard Cubic Metres per Day, a standard unit measuring daily natural gas flow and consumption volume.
PNG
Piped Natural Gas, natural gas delivered directly to kitchens, commercial establishments, and factories through an underground pipeline network.

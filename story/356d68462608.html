RBI grants in-principle payment aggregator approval to Axis Bank's Freecharge

A 34% surge in UPI transactions sets up Freecharge's FY27 merchant processing launch.

  • Freecharge received RBI in-principle approval to operate as a payment aggregator.
  • The nod covers PA-O1 and PA-P1 categories starting in financial year 2027.
  • Commercial launch timelines and final licence compliance milestones from the RBI.
Banking & FinancialsFintech & Payments

What happened

On October 17, 2026, the Reserve Bank of India granted in-principle approval to Axis Bank's digital payments platform, Freecharge, to operate as a payment aggregator. The regulatory clearance covers operations under the PA-O1 and PA-P1 categories beginning in FY27. This comes as Axis Bank reported a 34% year-on-year increase in UPI transaction volumes.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. RBI issues in-principle payment aggregator approval
  2. Freecharge completes technical audits and operational setup
  3. Axis Bank integrates merchant acquiring with existing payment flows
  4. Transaction processing fees begin accruing to fee-based income

The in-principle approval initiates a setup phase where the platform must satisfy regulatory and technical audit conditions before formal launch. Once commercial operations commence in FY27, Freecharge can onboard merchants directly to process multi-channel digital payments. This enables Axis Bank to capture transaction fees internally rather than routing merchant flows through third-party processors. Financial contribution will reflect in banking non-interest income over multiple quarters after commercial rollout.

Nifty IT logs biggest weekly jump since 2008 as index surges 11.7%

An 18-year weekly record in software shares masks regional semiconductor weakness.

  • Nifty IT gained 11.7% in its best weekly rally since 2008.
  • Strong quarterly earnings revived institutional buying across large-cap software and banking counters.

What happened

The Nifty IT index jumped 11.7% over the week ending October 17, 2026, marking its largest weekly gain in 18 years. Gains were propelled by better-than-expected quarterly earnings from firms like HCLTech, lifting the broader Nifty up 2.78% to reclaim its 200-week moving average. This domestic rally occurred even as Asian semiconductor stocks experienced notable declines.

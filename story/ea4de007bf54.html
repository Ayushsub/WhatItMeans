SEBI to ease FII rules and compliance to boost capital markets

Foreign funds may face simplified rules as the regulator targets heavy institutional outflows.

  • SEBI announced upcoming rules to ease compliance for foreign institutional investors and fundraising.
  • Simpler regulations aim to stem heavy foreign capital outflows and lower compliance costs.
  • The consultation paper on foreign investor compliance expected within weeks.
Financial ServicesInstitutional Flows

What happened

On October 31, 2026, SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey announced plans to release a consultation paper within weeks to streamline Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) compliance rules. The regulatory initiative aims to align domestic norms with global standards and cut compliance costs for market participants. The announcement comes against the backdrop of significant domestic equity fundraising alongside persistent foreign fund outflows.

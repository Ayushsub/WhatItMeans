Mid-size suvs gain market share in india as buyers shift to larger cars

Buyers now value space and premium features over compact cars

What happened

The Economic Times reports that mid-size SUVs are increasing their share of the Indian passenger‑vehicle market. Buyers are shifting preference from compact cars to larger vehicles that offer more space, comfort and advanced features.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: buyer preference shifts toward mid-size SUVs
  2. step 2: demand for larger, premium vehicles rises
  3. step 3: manufacturers increase SUV production and pricing
  4. step 4: earnings reflect higher revenue and average transaction price

The preference shift forces consumers to look for bigger, feature‑rich cars, which pushes manufacturers to allocate capacity to SUVs and command higher prices. Production adjustments take a few months, after which earnings show higher sales and improved margins. The effect on consumer prices may appear later as dealers pass on higher costs.

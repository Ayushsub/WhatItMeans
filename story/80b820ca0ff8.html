India signs eight free trade agreements boosting electronics, agriculture, gems exports

Eight new FTAs promise duty‑free entry for Indian electronics exporters

What happened

On September 4, 2026, India announced the signing of eight free trade agreements covering the European Union, United Kingdom and other partners. The deals provide duty‑free market access for Indian products, especially in electronics, agriculture and gems and jewellery sectors.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. Signing of eight FTAs creates duty‑free market access for Indian goods
  2. Tariff elimination forces lower export costs and improves price competitiveness abroad
  3. Improved competitiveness pressures foreign buyers to increase orders from Indian firms
  4. Higher order volumes translate into higher revenues and earnings for exporters, reflected in future financial results

The agreements first remove import duties, which forces exporters to face lower costs when selling abroad. This cost advantage pushes foreign buyers to prefer Indian products, raising order books. The increased orders then flow through to higher sales and profit figures that will appear in company earnings later. Each step typically unfolds over a few weeks to months as customs procedures adjust and buyers reorder.

Ntpc profit jumps 39% in q2 fy27, revenue up 21%

What happened

NTPC reported a 39% year‑on‑year increase in consolidated net profit to ₹7,030 crore for Q2 FY27. Total income rose 21% to ₹54,715.69 crore. The company also announced its first interim dividend for the fiscal year at 30% of face value.

SEBI to release new framework on Closing Auction Session after receiving feedback

Over 3,500 public comments will shape how India's stock market closes daily.

  • SEBI is preparing a new framework for the stock market's Closing Auction Session.
  • The rules will alter how final daily share prices are calculated and settled.
  • The release of the official circular detailing the final operational changes.
Capital Markets

What happened

On October 3, 2026, reports indicated that the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is preparing to release a new framework for the Closing Auction Session. The regulator received over 3,500 specific comments on its consultation paper regarding this mechanism, alongside thousands of other responses concerning market timings and derivatives settlement. Officials stated that these responses will be reviewed quickly before issuing the final circular.

Who is affected

institutional investors and day traders
They will face new rules on how final closing prices are determined at the end of the trading day.
retail stock market participants
They may experience changes in transaction execution and settlement timings during the final minutes of trade.

Which sectors

  • Capital Markets mixed — While clearer rules on closing auctions can reduce price manipulation, adjusting to new settlement and timing frameworks requires operational changes for brokerages.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. SEBI finalises and issues the new Closing Auction Session framework
  2. Stock exchanges alter the algorithm and timing rules for the final 30 minutes of trade
  3. Market participants adjust their order-matching and pricing strategies during the close
  4. Final daily closing prices of shares reflect more accurate demand-supply dynamics with lower volatility

Once the regulator issues the circular, stock exchanges will have a transition period of a few weeks to months to implement the new system. Brokerages and institutional trading desks must then update their software to align with the new auction rules. This chain ultimately alters how the final daily price of a stock is calculated, directly impacting derivative settlements and mutual fund net asset values (NAVs) which rely on these closing prices.

Jargon, in plain words

Closing Auction Session
A specific window at the end of the trading day where buy and sell orders are pooled to determine the final closing price of a stock.
Derivatives settlement
The process of fulfilling a futures or options contract, either by delivering the underlying asset or settling the price difference in cash.

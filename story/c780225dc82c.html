Gold drops over 3% as higher oil prices and US policy expectations weigh

Retail buyers in Chennai face ₹1,42,480 rates after a 3.85% drop ahead of Diwali.

  • Gold prices dropped over 3% in India during the third week of October 2026.

What happened

Gold prices dropped sharply in the week leading up to October 24, 2026, driven by elevated crude oil prices and expectations of tighter US central bank policy. MCX gold futures fell roughly 3.5%, while 24-karat retail gold in Chennai declined 3.85% to ₹1,42,480 per 10 grams.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. step 1: rising crude oil prices aggravate global inflation concerns
  2. step 2: central banks signal tighter monetary policy and higher interest rates
  3. step 3: higher expected yields raise the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding gold
  4. step 4: global and domestic bullion prices decline across futures and retail markets

Higher crude oil prices increase inflation fears, prompting expectations that the US Federal Reserve will maintain elevated interest rates. Because gold does not pay interest, higher yield expectations reduce its relative appeal over a period of days to weeks. This selling pressure transmits quickly into benchmark commodity futures contracts on exchanges like the MCX. Finally, local retail bullion markets in cities like Chennai adjust daily prices downwards to reflect international and futures benchmarks.

Market reaction

MCX gold futures dropped around 3.5% during the week, while retail 24K gold prices in Chennai fell 3.85% to ₹1,42,480 per 10 grams.

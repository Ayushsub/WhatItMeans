NBFC credit for gold jewellery jumped 66.1% in September, shows RBI data

NBFC credit for gold jewellery reached Rs 3.74 lakh crore after jumping 66.1%.

  • NBFC credit for gold jewellery and consumer durables grew over 50% in September.
  • Rapid retail loan growth boosts lender revenues but heightens central bank scrutiny.
  • Whether the RBI introduces stricter capital rules to curb fast-paced lending.

What happened

According to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) data released for September 2026, non-banking financial companies saw credit to gold jewellery surge 66.1% year-on-year to Rs 3.74 lakh crore. Credit extended for consumer durables also expanded rapidly, posting a 54.4% year-on-year increase over the same period. The uptick highlights strong consumer reliance on loan products to fund purchases and obtain personal liquidity.

SEBI to review settlement price methodology for derivatives amid CAS rollout

On September 5, 2026 SEBI announced a review of derivative pricing

What happened

On 5 September 2026 SEBI issued a press release stating it will review the settlement price methodology for derivative contracts. The review is linked to the rollout of the Centralized Automated Settlement (CAS) system. SEBI said the exercise aims to ensure pricing aligns with the new settlement infrastructure.

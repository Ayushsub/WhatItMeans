What happened

On Oct 17, 2026 traders noted spot gold fell 0.1% and futures edged lower, signalling a weekly drop. The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. step 1: Fed signals lower probability of a rate hike
  2. step 2: markets price a weaker dollar and softer Treasury yields
  3. step 3: a weaker dollar lowers gold prices and reduces rupee value of USD earnings for exporters
  4. step 4: lower gold costs help domestic buyers, while IT firms see earnings translate into fewer rupees

The Fed's dovish tone forces investors to downgrade dollar expectations, which pushes the currency down and eases yields on US Treasuries. The cheaper dollar depresses gold, a commodity priced in USD, benefiting Indian buyers. At the same time, Indian IT firms that invoice in dollars receive fewer rupees per dollar, hurting profit margins. These effects typically unfold over a few weeks as market pricing adjusts and earnings reports incorporate the new rates.

Public sector banks record 15-25% credit growth in q2 fy27

Five state banks lifted loan disbursements by up to a quarter

  • Credit growth of 15-25% reported for five PSBs in Q2 FY27.
  • Higher loan supply could boost earnings for banks and borrowers.
  • Watch RBI policy stance and credit quality trends.
bankingagricultureCredit & Liquidity

What happened

According to RBI sectoral data, five public sector banks posted credit growth between 15% and 25% in the second quarter of FY27. Demand for credit remained firm across agriculture, industry, services and personal loans.

agricultural loan borrowers

service sector borrowers
receive more financing, aiding consumption and investment, up 15-25%
personal loan borrowers
benefit from more loan approvals, volume rise 15-25%

Which sectors

  • banking tailwind — higher loan disbursements raise interest income
  • agriculture tailwind — greater credit flow supports farm financing

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. step 1: RBI data shows credit demand staying firm
  2. step 2: banks respond by expanding loan book
  3. step 3: borrowers obtain more funds at prevailing rates
  4. step 4: banks record higher interest earnings, reflecting in quarterly results

The RBI data signals strong demand, which forces banks to increase lending. This expands borrowers' access to credit at existing rates. The larger loan book raises banks' interest income, which will appear in their upcoming earnings. The effect may be seen with a lag of one to two quarters as loans are disbursed and interest accrues.

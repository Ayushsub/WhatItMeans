NCLT extends Supreme Infrastructure deadline to clear ₹2,200 crore debt

Lenders including SBI agreed on a framework for a ₹2,200 crore settlement.

  • NCLT extended the payment deadline to October 31 for Supreme Infrastructure.
  • Lenders including SBI and PNB agreed on a settlement framework.
  • Execution of payment obligations by Supreme Infrastructure under the agreed framework.
Banking & FinancialsReal Estate & Infra

What happened

The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Mumbai extended the payment deadline to October 31, 2026, for Supreme Infrastructure India. State Bank of India (SBI), Punjab National Bank (PNB), and Union Bank of India have agreed on a framework for the firm to complete its settlement obligations. The process aims to resolve outstanding debt obligations amounting to ₹2,200 crore.

Public sector lenders (SBI, PNB, Union Bank)

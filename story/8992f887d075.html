Indian banks offer personal loans starting at 8.90% in September 2026

Borrowers face varying processing fees and five-year EMI calculations on new personal loans.

  • Major Indian banks set starting personal loan interest rates at 8.90% in September 2026.
  • Borrowers' actual interest rates and monthly EMIs depend heavily on individual credit profiles.
  • What to watch is how processing fees and credit scores affect final borrowing costs.

What happened

In September 2026, major Indian public and private sector banks, including Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and State Bank of India, updated their personal loan offerings. The starting interest rates for these unsecured loans begin at 8.90% per annum. Borrowers are being presented with varying processing fees and five-year Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) options.

Retail borrowers seeking unsecured personal loans
They will see starting interest rates from 8.90%, but their final EMI depends on credit scores and processing fees.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. Step 1: Banks set competitive starting personal loan rates at 8.90%
  2. Step 2: Retail consumers compare these starting rates against processing fees and loan tenors
  3. Step 3: Banks assess individual borrower credit scores to determine the actual risk-adjusted interest rate
  4. Step 4: This final rate determines the monthly EMI paid by the borrower and the interest income earned by the bank

When banks advertise starting rates of 8.90%, it serves as an initial hook to attract retail borrowers. Within days of application, the bank evaluates the applicant's credit score and history, which typically forces a risk-adjusted premium onto the starting rate for average borrowers. This process determines the final Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) that the consumer must pay over the loan's tenure, usually ranging up to five years. Ultimately, these finalized loan agreements translate into interest income for banks, showing up in their quarterly earnings reports with a lag of one to two quarters.

Jargon, in plain words

EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment, which is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month.
Unsecured loan
A loan that is issued and supported only by the borrower's creditworthiness, rather than by any collateral like property or gold.

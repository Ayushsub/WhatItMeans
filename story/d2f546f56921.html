RBI sets 85% compensation rule for digital fraud victims from 2027

Victims can claim up to 85% of losses after filing by Dec 31 2026

  • RBI announced 85% loss compensation rule effective Jan 1 2027
  • Banks must cover most of victims' net loss
  • Watch filing deadlines and banks' fraud provisions
Banking & Financials

What happened

The Reserve Bank of India issued new guidelines that will take effect on 1 January 2027. Eligible victims of digital banking fraud will be entitled to compensation equal to 85% of their net loss. The rules also specify a complaint‑filing deadline, though the exact cutoff date is not detailed in the source.

The rule triggers a regulatory requirement that forces banks to allocate more capital for fraud losses. This allocation reduces available earnings in the short term and may lead banks to pass costs onto customers through higher fees or tighter credit terms. The impact typically appears in quarterly earnings as a rise in the fraud‑provision line item. The lag between the rule’s start and visible earnings impact is usually a few quarters.

Openai secures $11 billion funding, valuation rises to $1.25 trillion

What happened

OpenAI completed a funding round that raised $11 billion in its first tranche, pushing its post‑money valuation to $1.25 trillion. The round was scaled back from an earlier plan of over $30 billion amid a broader tech sell‑off and growing concerns about AI spending and regulation.

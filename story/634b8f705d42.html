India targets $50 billion telecom equipment exports, up from $1 billion

Export goal jumps from $1 bn to $50 bn, reshaping trade outlook

  • Telecom equipment exports currently $0.6‑1 bn
  • Aim is to reach $50 bn export value
  • Watch policy support and global demand

What happened

The Economic Times reports that India’s telecom equipment exports are about $0.6‑1 billion annually, while imports are four to five times larger. A new NITI Aayog assessment proposes turning this imbalance into an opportunity, targeting a $50 billion export sector. The report highlights the scale gap and the potential economic upside.

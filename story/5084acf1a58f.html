SEBI says current exchange infrastructure can handle higher volumes, regional exchanges face challenges

Investors in regional stock markets may lose local trading venues

  • SEBI confirms existing exchanges can absorb larger trade volumes
  • Location‑based exchanges become harder to run
  • Regulatory focus shifts to FPI compliance and director governance
financial servicesInstitutional Flows

What happened

On 31 October 2026 SEBI Chairman stated that the existing exchange infrastructure is sufficient to handle larger trading volumes. He added that operating location‑based exchanges is now difficult and that SEBI will soon release a consultation paper on foreign portfolio investor (FPI) compliance while boosting governance support for independent directors.

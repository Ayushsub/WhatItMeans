India government to discuss bulk scotch import tariffs with liquor firms

Domestic giants and foreign peers meet over 80% bulk Scotch imports

  • Government holds talks on bulk scotch tariffs
  • Potential cost shift for whisky makers

What happened

On 31 October 2026 the Indian government announced meetings with major liquor companies, including Diageo and Pernod, to discuss tariff reductions on bulk Scotch whisky imports. About 80% of Scotch arriving from Britain is in bulk form for local blending. Industry players have expressed alarm over the ongoing dispute with foreign peers.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: government signals possible tariff reduction on bulk Scotch imports
  2. step 2: reduced customs duty forces lower landed cost for bulk whisky
  3. step 3: manufacturers using bulk whisky can cut production costs or boost margins
  4. step 4: cost changes appear as either lower retail prices or higher earnings in quarterly results

The policy signal first alters the duty rate at customs, which takes effect within weeks. Importers then pay less for bulk Scotch, a change that passes through the supply chain over a month or two. Companies that blend Scotch can either reduce selling prices, stimulating demand, or retain the savings as higher profit margins, which will show up in earnings reports a quarter later. The lag is therefore a few weeks for cost transmission and up to a quarter for earnings impact.

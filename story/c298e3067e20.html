RBI approves LIC to acquire up to 9.99% stake in ICICI Bank

LIC secures a one-year window to buy a 9.99% stake in ICICI Bank.

  • RBI approved LIC to acquire up to 9.99% of ICICI Bank.
  • It signals LIC's growing appetite for major private sector banking assets.
  • The actual pace and size of LIC's share purchases over the next year.
Banking & Financials

What happened

On September 4, 2026, ICICI Bank received approval from the Reserve Bank of India allowing Life Insurance Corporation of India to increase its holding. Under this approval, LIC can acquire up to 9.99% of the bank's paid-up share capital. The state-run insurer has one year from the date of the RBI's letter to complete the acquisition.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. RBI grants approval for LIC to buy up to 9.99% of ICICI Bank
  2. LIC deploys capital to purchase shares from the open market
  3. Increased buying pressure absorbs floating stock of ICICI Bank
  4. The bank's valuation receives support, potentially lowering its cost of raising future equity capital

The process begins with the regulatory approval, which is valid for one year, meaning the market impact will not be instantaneous but spread out. As LIC gradually deploys its massive premium inflows to purchase shares, this steady demand absorbs available market supply over several months. This buying pressure typically stabilizes or boosts the stock price, which is reflected in the bank's market valuation. Ultimately, a stronger valuation makes it cheaper for the bank to raise equity capital in the future, though this benefit may take quarters to materialize.

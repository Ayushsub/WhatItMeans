Avenue Supermarts Q2 net profit rises 8.5% to ₹743 crore as cost pressures squeeze margins

DMart parent revenue rose 18% to ₹19,644 crore while profit growth lagged behind.

  • DMart parent Avenue Supermarts reported Q2 net profit of ₹743 crore, up 8.5%.
  • Rising operational expenses and entry-level wage inflation compressed operating margins despite 18% revenue growth.
  • Management comments on wage cost trajectory and store expansion strategy in upcoming quarters.
Organised RetailInflation

What happened

Avenue Supermarts reported an 8.5% year-on-year increase in consolidated net profit to ₹742.98 crore for the September 2026 quarter. Consolidated revenue from operations grew 17.8% year-on-year to ₹19,644 crore, compared to ₹16,676.30 crore in the same period last year. Revenue growth was supported by new store openings and stronger sales across mature stores, though rising operating costs slowed overall profit growth.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. Entry-level wage inflation increases employee compensation costs
  2. Store operational expenses expand faster than store-level sales efficiency
  3. EBITDA and profit after tax margins contract
  4. Net profit growth trails total top-line revenue growth

Wage increases for entry-level staff directly raise DMart's operational spending across its store network. Because labor costs rose faster than revenue per store, operating profitability margins compressed during the quarter. This cost structure pressure caused profit growth of 8.5% to fall behind revenue growth of 17.8%.

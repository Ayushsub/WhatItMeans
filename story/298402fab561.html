Nayara Energy hikes petrol by ₹5 and diesel by ₹3 across 7,108 pumps

Over 7,100 fuel stations now test customer tolerance against state-run competitors.

  • Nayara Energy raised petrol by ₹5 and diesel by ₹3 per litre.
  • Geopolitical conflict and rising crude prices squeeze margins for private fuel retailers.
  • Price differences between private outlets and state-run oil marketing companies.
Fuel retailingRoad logisticsCrude & Energy

What happened

Nayara Energy raised retail prices of petrol by ₹5 per litre and diesel by ₹3 per litre with immediate effect on October 3, 2026. The increase applies across its nationwide network of 7,108 fuel stations. The decision follows escalating geopolitical tensions and higher global crude and refined product costs that squeezed marketing margins.

Who is affected

Customers at Nayara fuel stations
Direct fuel cost increase of ₹5 per litre for petrol and ₹3 per litre for diesel.
State-run fuel retailers
Likely shift in consumer footfall towards public sector pumps if their pump prices remain unchanged.

  • Road logistics headwind — A ₹3 per litre increase in diesel immediately raises fleet operating costs for hauliers refuelling at private pumps.

International conflict produces an immediate spike in crude input costs and refinery gate fuel prices within days.

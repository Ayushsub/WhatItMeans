What happened

On 25 September 2026 MCX gold prices fell by ₹3,351 per 10 grams, turning September’s gains into losses. The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. step 1: US Treasury yields rise as Fed rate‑hike expectations increase
  2. step 2: higher yields strengthen the US dollar and raise the opportunity cost of holding non‑interest‑bearing assets like gold
  3. step 3: a stronger dollar and higher opportunity cost depress global gold demand, pulling down spot prices
  4. step 4: lower spot price translates into cheaper MCX contracts and reduced valuations for gold‑linked funds

The yield rise first forces the dollar higher, which makes gold less attractive compared with yield‑bearing assets. That demand shift pushes the global spot price down within a few days, which is reflected in MCX prices almost immediately. The price change then filters through to Indian investors and jewellery makers, with the impact on earnings appearing in the next quarterly reports. The lag from the yield move to earnings impact is typically a few weeks to a quarter.

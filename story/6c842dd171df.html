Anthropic eyes new model launch ahead of IPO as revenue nears $100 billion

A $2 billion safety pact accompanies an antitrust lawsuit challenging development pauses.

  • Anthropic plans a new model launch ahead of its planned public market debut.

What happened

Anthropic is preparing to launch a new artificial intelligence model ahead of an expected initial public offering to counter OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. The company expects its annualised revenue to top $100 billion in 2026 and announced a $2 billion partnership with Accenture for model safety evaluation. Simultaneously, Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, and Google were sued in California federal court for alleged antitrust violations regarding public calls to slow down AI development.

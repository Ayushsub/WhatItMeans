ADNOC explores expanded strategic fuel storage and long-term LPG deals with India

Over 500,000 daily crude barrels tie domestic energy security directly to Abu Dhabi talks.

  • ADNOC plans more long-term LPG supply agreements with India.
  • Bilateral supply covers roughly one in every ten barrels India imports.
  • Watch for final storage agreements between ADNOC and ISPRL.
Oil and Gas Refining & MarketingCrude & EnergyTrade & Tariffs

What happened

Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) announced on October 17, 2026, that it is open to additional long-term liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) supply deals with India. The company delivered more than 500,000 barrels of crude oil per day to India in 2025, accounting for nearly 10% of India's crude imports. ADNOC is also actively exploring opportunities with Indian Strategic Petroleum Reserves Limited (ISPRL) to expand domestic storage of crude oil, liquefied natural gas (LNG), and LPG.

Expanding storage partnerships with ADNOC immediately buffers physical energy reserves across Indian ports. Within months, bilateral long-term contracts replace variable spot purchases, lowering exposure to abrupt maritime freight and pricing spikes. Over several quarters, deeper strategic reserves provide Indian state refiners with bargaining power and price stability across commercial and domestic distribution networks.

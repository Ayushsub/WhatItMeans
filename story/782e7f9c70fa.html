What happened

On August 22, 2026, former UK Prime Minister Boris Johnson commented on the progress of the India-UK Free Trade Agreement (FTA), jokingly addressing missed deadlines by saying he never specified which Diwali the deal would be signed. Johnson highlighted a key breakthrough in the negotiations: a proposed reduction in the Indian import tariff on Scotch whisky from 150% to 75%. He noted that bilateral trade relations have continued to grow following the UK's exit from the European Union.

Plays out over quarters

  1. India reduces the import tariff on Scotch whisky from 150% to 75% under the FTA.
  2. Importers pay lower landing costs for British spirits at Indian ports.
  3. Distributors pass these savings down to retail shops, hotels, and restaurants.
  4. Lower retail prices stimulate consumer demand, boosting sales volumes and earnings for premium beverage companies.

The process begins when the tariff cut from 150% to 75% is officially implemented, which typically takes several months after an FTA is signed due to legislative procedures. Once active, the immediate drop in customs duty directly lowers the landed cost of Scotch whisky for Indian importers.

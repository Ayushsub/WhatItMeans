30% margin cap on 137 cancer drugs could cut prices up to 70%

What happened

On 9 November 2026, India’s National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) imposed a 30% cap on trade margins for 137 anti‑cancer medicines, including Atezolizumab and Bevacizumab. The rule forces manufacturers and distributors to adjust prices, with analysts estimating price reductions of up to 70%.

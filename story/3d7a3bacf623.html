BSE Closing Auction Session triggers liquidity drop in options, Nuvama reports

Falling option premium volumes on BSE threaten derivatives market depth and participation.

  • BSE's new Closing Auction Session triggered sharp declines in option volumes.
  • Thin liquidity risks creating a self-reinforcing cycle of falling derivatives activity.
  • Watch for regulatory clarity and exchange steps to improve closing auction participation.
Capital Markets & ExchangesCredit & Liquidity

What happened

BSE's new Closing Auction Session (CAS) has witnessed low market participation, leading to sharp declines in option premium volumes and contract turnover, according to a report by Nuvama. The drop in activity has triggered a self-reinforcing liquidity spiral amid operational confusion and retail investor concerns. The mechanism has also faced increased scrutiny over alleged price manipulation during the closing window.

Who is affected

Derivatives traders
Face higher execution costs and wider bid-ask spreads due to thinner contract liquidity.
BSE and retail brokers
Risk a decline in transaction fee revenue and brokerage commissions if option turnover remains depressed.

Which sectors

  • Capital Markets & Exchanges headwind — Depressed option premium turnover and trading volumes compress fee pools for exchanges and discount brokerages.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over days

  1. BSE introduces new Closing Auction Session
  2. Participants encounter operational friction and thin initial depth
  3. Option premium volumes and open contracts fall sharply
  4. Wider bid-ask spreads discourage traders, perpetuating lower volume

The introduction of BSE's Closing Auction Session altered the price discovery mechanism at market close. Early confusion and low trading participation immediately compressed transaction volumes across option contracts. Because thinner liquidity raises trading costs and execution risk, market participants pulled back further over subsequent days. This friction creates a compounding loop that suppresses derivatives activity until higher participation restores market depth.

Jargon, in plain words

Closing Auction Session (CAS)
A dedicated trading window at the end of the market day where buy and sell orders are pooled together to determine a single closing price.
Option premium volume
The total rupee value paid by traders to purchase options contracts, reflecting active trading turnover.

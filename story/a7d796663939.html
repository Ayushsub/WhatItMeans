Gold and silver slip as US jobs data fuels rate hike expectations

Gold fell ₹1,700 to ₹1,42,930 after 165,000 jobs added

  • Gold and silver prices dropped sharply
  • Strong US payrolls raise Fed hike odds

What happened

US nonfarm payrolls rose by 165,000 jobs in November, well above forecasts. The surprise lift pushed expectations that the Federal Reserve will raise its policy rate in December. Consequently, spot gold slipped ₹1,700 to ₹1,42,930 per 10 grams and silver fell 1.26% to ₹1,84,800 per kilogram.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over days

  1. step 1: US payrolls beat expectations
  2. step 2: markets price higher probability of a Fed rate hike
  3. step 3: the dollar strengthens and Treasury yields rise
  4. step 4: higher dollar and yields make gold less attractive, pulling prices down

The stronger‑than‑expected jobs report forces investors to anticipate tighter monetary policy. That expectation pushes the dollar index up and Treasury yields higher, both of which increase the opportunity cost of holding non‑yielding assets like gold. As a result, demand for gold and silver falls, translating into lower spot prices within a few days. The lag from the payroll release to price movement is typically 1‑3 days.

Market reaction

The articles report that gold fell ₹1,700 to ₹1,42,930 per 10 grams, silver slipped 1.26% to ₹1,84,800 per kilogram, and the US dollar index jumped sharply after the payroll data.

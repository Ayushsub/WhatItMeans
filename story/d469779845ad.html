When market participants sense that AI valuations are overstretched, they tend to sell those stocks, forcing prices down. That selling pressure pushes investors toward assets that historically protect against rising prices, such as commodities or inflation‑linked bonds. The shift typically unfolds over a few months as sentiment changes and portfolio rebalancing occurs.

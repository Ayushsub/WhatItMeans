Assam tea incentive scheme adds Rs 3/kg bonus for single‑origin exports

Rs 3 per kilogram bonus lifts GTAC‑linked CTC tea export margins

  • Assam offers Rs 3/kg bonus for single‑origin tea exports.
  • Export subsidies for Orthodox and Specialty teas are also increased.
  • The move aims to boost GTAC‑linked CTC export volumes.
agriculture/teaTrade & Tariffs

What happened

On September 19, 2026 the Assam government announced a revision to its ATISIS, adding a Rs 3 per kilogram bonus for single‑origin Assam tea exports. The revision also raised subsidies for Orthodox and Specialty tea categories, targeting the GTAC‑linked CTC segment. The changes are intended to make Indian tea more competitive abroad.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: revised ATISIS introduces Rs 3/kg bonus and higher subsidies
  2. step 2: exporters face lower effective export costs, making Indian tea cheaper overseas
  3. step 3: overseas buyers increase orders of GTAC‑linked CTC tea
  4. step 4: exporters and growers record higher revenues and profit margins

The policy change lowers the cost base for exporters by the bonus amount. This cost advantage can make Indian CTC tea prices more competitive overseas, prompting buyers to shift orders. As volumes rise, growers and exporters may see higher receipts, depending on market conditions. The effect unfolds over a few months as contracts are renegotiated and shipments are dispatched.

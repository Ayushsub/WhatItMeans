Chhattisgarh signs PM GatiShakti pacts to boost logistics and digital planning

Chhattisgarh integrates 35 state departments into digital logistics mapping systems.

  • Chhattisgarh signed pacts with BISAG-N and NLDSL under PM GatiShakti.
  • Digital mapping across 35 departments aims to cut logistics planning delays.
  • Track industrial investment flows and project clearance timelines across the state.
Logistics and InfrastructureManufacturingGrowth

What happened

Chhattisgarh entered into agreements with BISAG-N and NLDSL to expand the use of geospatial technology in infrastructure development. Over 35 state government departments have been onboarded onto the PM GatiShakti platform. The initiative will deploy geographic information system tools for route planning, road identification, and industrial connectivity.

Who is affected

Infrastructure and logistics developers
Faster project approvals and clearer land data reduce survey timelines and planning costs.
Industrial investors in Chhattisgarh
Access to mapped utility and freight corridors provides better visibility for setting up manufacturing units.

Which sectors

  • Logistics and Infrastructure tailwind — Integrated geospatial mapping streamlines multimodal project design and reduces execution bottlenecks.
  • Manufacturing tailwind — Better road and logistics connectivity lowers transport turnaround times for raw materials.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. State departments integrate mapping data into central PM GatiShakti portals
  2. Agencies identify connectivity gaps and overlapping rights-of-way during pre-planning
  3. Faster clearances reduce project gestation periods and capital idling for builders
  4. Lower logistics overheads enhance state-level manufacturing competitiveness over time

Standardising land and route data through BISAG-N and NLDSL allows inter-departmental visibility during project planning. In the near term of several months, this reduces the time spent on environmental and right-of-way clearances. Over subsequent quarters, civil engineering contracts face fewer mid-construction route revisions, curbing cost overruns. Over years, streamlined freight movement lowers operational logistics costs for industrial facilities across the state.

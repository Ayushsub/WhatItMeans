SEBI orders Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy to refund over ₹546 crore

Trading academy students face a five-month timeline to claim ₹546 crore in refunds.

  • SEBI ordered Avadhut Sathe to refund ₹546 crore collected for unregistered advisory services.
  • Regulators are clamping down heavily on financial influencers masking advisory operations as education.
  • Watch whether penalties and refund orders are contested before the appellate tribunal.
Financial education and advisoryRegistered wealth management

What happened

On October 24, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) directed Avadhut Sathe and his trading academy to refund ₹546.17 crore along with ₹67.87 crore in interest to investors within five months. The regulator found that the entity offered unregistered investment advisory and research services under the guise of training courses while using misleading testimonials. SEBI additionally slapped a penalty of ₹11.5 crore on the academy and its founder.

Who is affected

Students and course subscribers
Eligible participants stand to recover course fees totaling ₹546.17 crore plus interest within five months.
Unregistered financial course creators
Operators face heightened enforcement risk and potential disgorgement of all course-related subscription revenues.

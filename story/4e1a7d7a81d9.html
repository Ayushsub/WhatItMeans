Jio platforms IPO draws 7.4 lakh crore bids, 22.19x subscription

What happened

The Jio Platforms IPO, sized at ₹30,213 crore, opened on October 21 and was fully subscribed within about 100 minutes. Bids totalled roughly ₹7.41 lakh crore, giving a subscription ratio of 22.19×. Allotment results are slated for October 26, with listing expected on October 28.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. step 1: record‑high investor demand pushes subscription to 22.19×
  2. step 2: forces Jio to raise ₹30,213 crore at the upper price band
  3. step 3: inflates Jio’s post‑issue market capitalisation to about ₹10.30 lakh crore
  4. step 4: larger balance sheet enables accelerated 5G rollout and content spend, pressuring rivals
  5. step 5: investors see heightened earnings expectations, which may lift share prices at listing

The surge in demand forced the issue to be oversubscribed, compelling Jio to accept the top of its price band and raise the planned capital. That capital expands Jio’s market value, giving it resources to invest heavily in 5G and digital platforms, which in turn puts competitive pressure on other telecom players. The market anticipates higher future earnings, so the expectation shows up as price appreciation when the shares list, though the effect may lag by a few weeks as the rollout proceeds.

SEBI proposes two options for expiry‑day derivatives settlement and auction timing

Five new rules target the 30‑minute closing auction on Sep 12

  • SEBI released a consultation on new CAS rules
  • Changes affect how expiry‑day derivatives settle
  • Watch which settlement method is adopted
financial servicesCredit & Liquidity

What happened

On 12 September 2026 SEBI issued a consultation paper proposing five major changes to the Closing Auction Session (CAS). It outlined two alternatives for expiry‑day settlement prices: retain the current VWAP‑based method for the last 30 minutes of continuous trading, or adopt a blended VWAP‑plus‑closing‑auction price after at least one year. The paper also suggested adjusting the timing of the continuous trading session and other operational tweaks.

The regulator's proposal (step 1) compels exchanges to modify the price‑determination algorithm (step 2). This forces traders to recalibrate models that price futures and options, and brokers to re‑program order‑flow logic (step 3). The adjustments appear as changes in bid‑ask spreads, settlement‑day price swings and the profitability of trading desks, which will be reflected in quarterly results (step 4). The lag is short for system changes (weeks) but market‑behaviour effects may take months to materialise.

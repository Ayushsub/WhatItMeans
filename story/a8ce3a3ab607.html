Kotak Mahindra Bank Q2 consolidated profit rises 26% to Rs 5,645 crore

A drop in bad loans to 1.04% shifted quarterly earnings above market estimates.

  • Kotak Mahindra Bank posted a 26% rise in Q2 consolidated net profit.
  • Lower bad-loan ratios and 16% income growth signal stable private banking asset health.
  • Credit growth durability and funding costs across subsequent quarters.
Banking & FinancialsCredit & Liquidity

What happened

On October 24, 2026, Kotak Mahindra Bank reported a 26% year-on-year increase in consolidated net profit to Rs 5,645 crore for the July-September quarter. Standalone profit after tax rose 29% to Rs 4,211 crore, supported by a 16% growth in net interest income. Gross non-performing assets improved over the period, falling to 1.04% from 1.39% a year earlier.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. Credit recoveries and controlled slippages lower gross NPAs to 1.04%
  2. Lender sets aside less money for potential bad debts
  3. Net interest income advances 16% alongside lower loan provisioning
  4. Standalone and consolidated bottom-line profits expand by 29% and 26%

Over the quarter, improved collections and asset monitoring lowered delinquent loans from 1.39% to 1.04%. Within weeks of these recoveries, the bank reduced the provisioning buffer it must set aside against stressed assets. This reduction, combined with a 16% growth in core lending income, immediately flowed into reported operating margins. By the end of the quarterly reporting cycle, this mechanism resulted in standalone net profit rising 29% to Rs 4,211 crore.

Jargon, in plain words

Net interest income (NII)
The difference between the total interest a bank earns from borrowers and the interest it pays out to depositors.
Gross non-performing assets (GNPA)
The total value of loans that borrowers have stopped repaying for at least 90 days, expressed as a share of total lending.

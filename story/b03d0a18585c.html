The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: NTPC's export announcement signals commercial intent for green hydrogen
  2. step 2: This encourages investment in electrolyzer capacity and related supply chains
  3. step 3: Increased production lowers per‑unit costs and creates export contracts with Asian/European buyers

The announcement acts as a market signal, prompting firms to fund electrolyzer projects. Those projects take several months to become operational, after which production ramps up and unit costs fall. Export contracts then lock in revenue streams, which appear in NTPC's earnings and could influence domestic power tariffs if capacity is shifted abroad. The lag between investment and earnings impact spans a few months to a year.

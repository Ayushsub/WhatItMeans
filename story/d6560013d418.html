IRDAI pushes insurance reform feedback deadline to Oct 31

Over 650 stakeholders have already weighed in on the reforms

  • Deadline extended to Oct 31
  • More time may reshape final rules
  • Watch for final reform announcement
Insurance

What happened

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) announced on Oct 24 that the feedback window for its proposed insurance distribution reforms has been moved from Oct 25 to Oct 31, 2026. The regulator noted that it has received more than 650 comments on the draft reforms so far. The extension aims to give additional time for stakeholders to submit observations before the final consultation closes.

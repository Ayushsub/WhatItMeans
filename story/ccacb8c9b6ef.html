Nifty stays around 24,250 as West Asia tensions lift crude prices

What happened

On August 22, 2026 the Nifty index closed at 24,252, down 0.47% from the previous session. The move came amid heightened West Asia tensions that pushed crude oil prices higher and kept market volatility low. Analysts noted the index is likely to remain confined between 23,900‑24,100 support and 24,450‑24,750 resistance.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. West Asia tensions raise crude oil prices
  2. Higher oil lifts input‑cost and inflation expectations
  3. Investors adopt cautious stance, keeping volatility low
  4. Nifty trades within narrow support‑resistance band, limiting earnings surprises

The geopolitical flare‑up pushes crude up, which feeds into cost pressures for companies and fuels inflation worries. Those concerns make investors risk‑averse, so market volatility stays subdued. With low volatility, option pricing is cheap and traders avoid large bets, leaving the Nifty confined to its technical range. The effect shows up as flat price movement and modest earnings guidance for most listed firms. The lag from oil price to index movement is typically a few days to a week.

Nifty slipped 0.47% to 24,252 and remained rangebound, with analysts noting subdued volatility and limited trading activity.

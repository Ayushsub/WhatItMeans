What happened

On November 6, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) announced the commencement of the SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026. The notification brings into force updated rules governing how administrative and civil proceedings can be settled with the capital markets regulator.

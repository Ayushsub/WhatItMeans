States announce DA hikes as Centre weighs pay revision for employees

Millions of pensioners await Cabinet decisions after six states released festive allowance hikes.

  • Several states raised dearness allowance while the Centre considers a July 2026 revision.
  • Higher allowances lift government workers' disposable income ahead of key festive spending periods.
  • Official Cabinet announcements regarding Central employee dearness relief expected around October 28.
FMCG and retailState finances

What happened

Multiple state governments, including Himachal Pradesh, Kerala, Telangana, Sikkim, Punjab, and Andhra Pradesh, announced dearness allowance (DA) increases in 2026. In Uttar Pradesh, employees are awaiting a July 2026 hike after the state previously raised DA from 58% to 60% in May. Meanwhile, Central government employees and pensioners anticipate an official announcement on their July 2026 DA and dearness relief revisions following an upcoming Cabinet meeting expected on October 28, 2026.

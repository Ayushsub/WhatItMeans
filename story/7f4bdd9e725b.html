AI volumes expected to outpace revenue deflation in Indian IT by FY30

CLSA projects AI-driven project volumes to exceed revenue shrinkage by FY30

  • CLSA sees AI volumes offsetting revenue decline
  • AI could boost IT earnings despite headwinds
  • Watch AI adoption pace and revenue trends
IT & TechInflation

What happened

CLSA reported that the Indian IT sector faces multiple macro and micro headwinds. It said AI adoption may initially compress revenues as productivity gains are passed to clients. However, the firm expects AI-driven project volumes to outweigh the revenue deflation by fiscal year 2030 (FY30).

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. step 1: AI adoption accelerates, generating more AI‑related project orders
  2. step 2: firms allocate resources to meet higher volumes, mitigating per‑project price pressure
  3. step 3: increased billings from AI projects lift top‑line growth despite overall revenue deflation
  4. step 4: earnings reflect higher AI‑driven revenue in FY30

The trigger is faster AI adoption, which forces companies to take on more AI projects. This resource allocation counters the revenue compression from lower pricing, so the effect reaches company earnings a few quarters later as AI billings rise. The lag is typically several quarters as projects move from win to delivery and invoicing.

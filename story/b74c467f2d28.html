Uno Minda plans ₹1,415 crore capex for auto component plants in India

A ₹1,415 crore manufacturing expansion targets rising demand for Indian auto components.

  • Uno Minda announced a ₹1,415 crore capital expenditure plan to expand manufacturing.
  • Increased capacity in Tamil Nadu, Haryana, and Bangalore addresses growing automotive component demand.
  • Watch the timeline of plant commissionings and subsequent volume growth in the auto sector.
Auto ComponentsMetals & MiningFiscal & Budget

What happened

On September 15, 2026, auto component manufacturer Uno Minda announced a capital expenditure plan of ₹1,415 crore to expand its domestic manufacturing capacity. The investment will fund new aluminium casting and alloy wheel plants in Tamil Nadu and Haryana, alongside expanding a moulding facility in Bangalore. These projects aim to meet rising demand for two-wheeler alloy wheels, interior and exterior parts, and sealing systems.

Jargon, in plain words

alloy wheels
Automotive wheels made from an alloy of aluminium or magnesium, which are lighter and dissipate heat better than standard steel wheels.
casting
A manufacturing process where liquid metal is poured into a mold to solidify into a specific shape.

What happened

On 7 November 2026 the Reserve Bank of India issued revised gold loan regulations. The rules raise loan‑to‑value (LTV) limits for small borrowers, mandate transparent gold valuation and introduce stricter auction safeguards, along with new repayment, jewellery return and compensation provisions.

Bank of Baroda hikes 555-day deposit rates by 10 bps to 7.50%

Depositors in Bank of Baroda's 555-day special scheme can earn up to 7.50% interest.

  • Bank of Baroda raised interest rates on its 555-day deposit scheme by 10 bps.
  • Higher deposit rates attract retail funds but elevate the bank's interest expenditure.
  • Monitor whether competing public sector lenders follow with similar deposit rate hikes.
Banking & FinancialsInterest Rates

What happened

Bank of Baroda raised interest rates on its 555-day deposit scheme by 10 basis points on November 12, 2026, taking the maximum return to 7.50% per annum. For general customers, including NRE and NRO account holders, the revised annual interest rates stand at 6.85% for callable deposits and 6.90% for non-callable deposits.

Who is affected

Bank of Baroda fixed deposit holders
Investors locking money into the 555-day tenure receive an additional 0.10% annual return.
Bank of Baroda equity shareholders
Higher deposit payout rates modestly increase the bank's interest expenses.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. step 1: Bank of Baroda increases interest rate on 555-day deposits by 10 basis points
  2. step 2: Higher yields incentivize retail and non-resident account holders to park funds
  3. step 3: Bank secures longer-duration liabilities while its weighted average cost of deposits rises
  4. step 4: Elevated funding costs apply mild downward pressure on net interest margins

By raising yields on special-tenure fixed deposits, Bank of Baroda incentivizes retail savers and non-resident depositors to lock in funds for 555 days. Over the coming weeks, this drives deposit inflows, helping the bank fund its credit expansion and manage liquidity. However, paying higher interest rates increases the bank's total cost of liabilities, which can compress its net interest margin over subsequent quarters unless loan interest rates are also adjusted upward.

Jargon, in plain words

Basis points (bps)
A financial unit of measurement where 100 basis points equal 1 percentage point; 10 bps equals 0.10%.
Callable deposit
A fixed deposit that allows the investor to withdraw money before the full maturity period ends.
Non-callable deposit
A fixed deposit where premature withdrawal before maturity is restricted, usually offering a slightly higher interest rate in exchange.

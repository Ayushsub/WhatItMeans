What happened

On 19 September 2026, Elon Musk said AI could double US GDP growth to 4% next year, according to a Dolat Capital report. The comment was published by both Economic Times and The Hindu BusinessLine. The report also warned that AI investment faces significant capital hurdles.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. step 1: US firms increase AI spending as productivity gains are projected
  2. step 2: higher US software and cloud demand pushes firms to outsource to cost‑effective providers
  3. step 3: Indian IT companies win more contracts, expanding revenues
  4. step 4: earnings growth appears in quarterly reports and may lift share prices

The AI spending surge forces US corporations to seek scalable tech services, which they often source from Indian firms due to cost advantage. This demand translates into larger order books for Indian IT players, showing up in their earnings a few quarters later. The lag is mainly the contract negotiation and project delivery phase, typically 2‑4 quarters. If capital constraints slow AI rollout, the chain weakens at step 1, limiting downstream benefits.

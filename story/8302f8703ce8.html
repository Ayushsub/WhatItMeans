10-year US Treasury yield hits 19-year high

US 10-year yield reaches 19-year high, pressuring global rates

  • 10-year Treasury yield climbs to 19-year high
  • Higher rates affect global financing and currency dynamics
  • Watch US inflation, Fed policy, and rupee movements
IT & TechInflation

What happened

On September 26, 2026 CNBC reported that the benchmark 10-year US Treasury yield rose to its highest level in 19 years. The climb was driven by persistent (sticky) inflation, a surge in bond issuance, and an investment boom linked to artificial intelligence (AI). The article highlighted that this marks the steepest rise since the early 2000s.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: US 10-year Treasury yield spikes to a 19-year high
  2. step 2: the rise pushes up global borrowing costs and strengthens the US dollar
  3. step 3: Indian firms feel tighter credit conditions and see their USD‑denominated revenues become more valuable
  4. step 4: earnings may show higher revenue volatility and margin pressure from costlier financing

The yield jump forces investors to demand higher returns, which lifts the dollar and raises the cost of borrowing worldwide. Indian IT firms, which invoice mainly in dollars, benefit from a stronger currency but also face higher interest rates on any dollar‑denominated debt. The net effect on earnings depends on how quickly US clients adjust their tech spend. The impact typically unfolds over several months as contracts are renegotiated and financing costs settle.

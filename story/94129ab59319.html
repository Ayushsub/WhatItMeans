Finance ministry urges broking firms to abandon family‑firm model

Brokers face a push to restructure ownership after ministry speech

  • Finance ministry calls for ownership changes
  • Aims to boost market trust

banking & financials

What happened

On 24 October 2026, Dr Alok Tiwari, Joint Secretary of the Ministry of Finance, told an ANMI‑NSE gathering that the government wants brokerage houses to move away from the family‑firm model and adopt structures that enhance structural trust. The remarks were part of a broader effort to tighten governance in the broking sector.

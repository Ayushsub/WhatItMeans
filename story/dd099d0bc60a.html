Uniflow expands control-valve capacity fivefold to cut import delays

Indian industrial projects face wait times of up to 40 weeks for imported valves.

  • Uniflow is expanding its control-valve manufacturing capacity fivefold to meet domestic demand.

What happened

Uniflow is expanding its manufacturing capacity for high-specification control valves by five times. This expansion comes as India attempts to reduce its 40% import reliance on these critical industrial components. Currently, procuring these specialized valves from overseas can take up to 40 weeks, creating bottlenecks in the domestic investment cycle.

domestic industrial project developers

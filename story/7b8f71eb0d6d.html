Canada expects india trade pact by year-end focusing on energy

Canada's trade minister cites upcoming LNG talks as key to deal

What happened

On 19 September 2026, Canada’s trade minister Maninder Sidhu said Canada is optimistic about concluding a comprehensive economic partnership agreement with India by the end of 2026. The negotiations are centred on the energy sector, notably liquefied natural gas (LNG) projects.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: Canada‑India trade talks progress toward a formal agreement
  2. step 2: agreement likely sets favourable tariffs and regulatory pathways for LNG trade
  3. step 3: reduced trade barriers encourage Canadian exporters to ship more LNG to India
  4. step 4: increased LNG imports affect Indian energy company earnings and may temper domestic gas prices

The negotiations act as the trigger; if an agreement is signed, it forces governments to align tariffs and customs procedures, which in turn lowers costs for exporters. Those cost savings translate into higher shipment volumes, showing up as higher revenue for Canadian LNG firms and potentially lower wholesale gas prices for Indian utilities. The lag between signing and actual shipments is typically several months as contracts are negotiated and cargoes scheduled.

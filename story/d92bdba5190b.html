PFRDA plans mobile pension accounts for informal workers via UPI and e-Shram

Millions of unregistered laborers could soon build retirement savings directly from their mobile phones.

  • PFRDA plans to let informal workers open pension accounts via mobile phones.
  • It simplifies retirement savings for millions of unorganized workers using UPI.
  • The rollout of the digital platform and integration with the e-Shram database.
Asset Management and Pension FundsFinancial Technology and Digital Payments

What happened

On September 12, 2026, the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) announced plans to allow informal sector workers to open pension accounts using mobile phones. According to PFRDA Chairman S Ramann, the system will leverage the existing e-Shram database of unorganized workers for account setup. Users will be able to make micro-contributions to their pension accounts using Unified Payments Interface (UPI).

Who is affected

Informal sector workers registered on the e-Shram portal
They gain a simplified, mobile-first pathway to start saving for retirement with small, flexible UPI payments.
Pension fund managers
They could see a substantial increase in assets under management as millions of new micro-accounts open.

Which sectors

  • Asset Management and Pension Funds tailwind — A massive influx of new retail pension accounts will increase the total pool of investable retirement assets over time.
  • Financial Technology and Digital Payments tailwind — Increased transaction volumes are expected as informal workers use UPI for recurring micro-contributions to their pension accounts.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. PFRDA launches the mobile-based pension enrollment system linked to e-Shram
  2. Informal workers complete digital onboarding on their mobile phones
  3. Workers initiate recurring micro-contributions using UPI
  4. Pension funds receive steady, long-term capital inflows to invest in debt and equity markets

The integration of the e-Shram database removes the paperwork barrier for unorganized workers, allowing instant identity verification. By enabling UPI, the system allows workers with irregular daily or weekly wages to contribute small amounts whenever they have surplus cash. This steady stream of micro-savings accumulates over months and quarters, gradually flowing into pension fund managers who invest these pools into broader financial markets.

Jargon, in plain words

PFRDA
Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority, the government body that supervises and regulates pensions in India.
e-Shram
A centralized database created by the Indian government to register and track unorganized sector workers to deliver social security benefits.
UPI
Unified Payments Interface, a real-time payment system in India that allows instant money transfers between bank accounts via mobile apps.

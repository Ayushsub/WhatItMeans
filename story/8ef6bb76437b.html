What happened

On Oct 3, 2026 RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra highlighted rising financial vulnerabilities stemming from geopolitical tensions, potential cyber‑attacks and cryptocurrency concerns. He outlined five policy priorities and urged vigilance. Parallel statements from finance minister Nirmala Sitharaman and market analysts reaffirmed a growth outlook around 7% for the year.

What happened

On 22 August 2026 the RBI released a calendar allowing premature redemption of sovereign gold bonds (SGB) from October 2026 through March 2027. The schedule covers 32 eligible tranches. Holders must submit redemption requests within the specified window to exit before the bonds mature.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: RBI publishes the redemption calendar
  2. step 2: investors submit redemption requests within the window
  3. step 3: RBI pays out principal, reducing outstanding SGBs
  4. step 4: cash flows alter liquidity and may shift funds to other assets, influencing gold and bond markets

The trigger is the RBI's calendar announcement, which forces investors to decide whether to redeem. Those who opt in submit requests, compelling the RBI to disburse cash and retire the corresponding SGB tranche. The resulting cash outflow reduces the supply of gold‑linked bonds and frees capital that can flow into alternative assets such as physical gold or other securities. Over the redemption window, earnings of banks and the pricing of gold may reflect these shifts, with a lag of weeks to months as funds are redeployed.

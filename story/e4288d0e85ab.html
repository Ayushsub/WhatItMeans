What happened

San Francisco Fed President Mary Daly said on Nov 6 that the recent jobs report was "good news for American workers" but stressed that inflation is still high. The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: fed signals another rate hike
  2. step 2: higher us policy rate pushes the dollar up
  3. step 3: a stronger dollar tends to weaken the rupee
  4. step 4: weaker rupee boosts export margins but raises import‑related costs

The Fed's hint of a rate increase forces market participants to price in higher US yields, which lifts the dollar. The currency move usually lags by a few days to weeks, then translates into a weaker rupee. Export‑focused firms feel the benefit relatively quickly in earnings, while import‑dependent companies see cost pressure that may take a few quarters to show up in profit margins.

FPIs pull out ₹35,860 crore from Indian equities in September

Foreign funds pulled ₹35,860 crore despite SEBI introducing simplified market access regulations.

  • Foreign portfolio investors sold ₹35,860 crore in Indian equities in September.
  • Regulatory easing alone cannot offset global competition and valuation concerns.
  • Foreign institutional flow trends and global monetary conditions in coming weeks.

What happened

Foreign portfolio investors sold a net ₹35,860 crore worth of Indian equities in September 2026, reversing inflows from the prior month. The sustained selling occurred despite regulatory initiatives by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to ease operational market access. Outflows were primarily driven by heightened global competition and persistent concerns regarding relative equity returns.

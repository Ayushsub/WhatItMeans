India-Germany trade ties deepen as German firms target multi-fold growth in 3 to 5 years

German firms targeting triple growth in India over five years could reshape local manufacturing.

  • Piyush Goyal concluded a two-day visit to Germany to expand trade and R&D ties.
  • India aims for trade pacts covering 75% of global GDP within 12 to 18 months.

What happened

Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal concluded a two-day visit to Germany on October 23, 2026, holding talks with business leaders, research institutes, and government officials. Goyal highlighted that India's trade agreements currently cover two-thirds of global markets, with plans to expand coverage to 75% of global GDP over the next 12 to 18 months. German businesses expressed intentions to double or triple their growth in India over the next three to five years through joint R&D and manufacturing partnerships.

India reimposes anti-dumping duty on Chinese titanium dioxide imports

Five-year levies of up to $681 per tonne directly alter raw material costs.

  • India reimposed anti-dumping duties of $460 to $681 per tonne on Chinese titanium dioxide.
  • Higher import costs protect domestic chemical makers while increasing input expenses for paint producers.
  • Pass-through of raw material cost hikes to consumer paint and coating prices over coming quarters.
Chemical ManufacturingPaints and CoatingsTrade & Tariffs

What happened

On October 24, 2026, the Finance Ministry notified anti-dumping duties ranging from $460 to $681 per tonne on specified titanium dioxide imports from China. The duty has been reimposed for five years following a fresh investigation into domestic industry injury. The probe concluded that dumped Chinese imports caused material harm to local manufacturers.

Who is affected

Domestic titanium dioxide manufacturers
Protection from cheap imports allows higher domestic market share and pricing power.
Paint and coatings manufacturers
Raw material procurement costs increase significantly for essential white pigment inputs.

Which sectors

  • Chemical Manufacturing tailwind — Domestic producers gain protection against underpriced imports from China.
  • Paints and Coatings headwind — Titanium dioxide is a key raw material whose higher landed import cost increases input expenses.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. Government levies $460-$681 per tonne duty on Chinese titanium dioxide
  2. Landed import price of foreign titanium dioxide rises significantly
  3. Industrial buyers face higher raw material procurement costs for pigments
  4. Profit margins compress unless costs are passed along to retail end-users

The anti-dumping notification instantly increases the landed cost of Chinese titanium dioxide by $460 to $681 per tonne. Over weeks to months, Indian chemical producers can capture higher market share as cheap foreign alternatives become uncompetitive. Consequently, paint and industrial coating companies incur higher raw material expenses, which may weigh on operating margins or lead to finished product price hikes over subsequent quarters.

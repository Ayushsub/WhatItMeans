What happened

On Oct 10, 2026, economic experts told the Economic Times that India must pursue open trade, strengthen its position in the World Trade Organisation (WTO), and play a more proactive global role. Their comments came as India posted a quarterly growth print of 2.6%. The remarks were framed as a strategic response to sustain that growth momentum.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over months

  1. step 1: experts publicly call for trade liberalisation
  2. step 2: policy makers feel pressure to reduce tariffs and negotiate wto reforms
  3. step 3: lower trade barriers lower costs for exporters and importers
  4. step 4: companies report higher export margins and consumers see lower prices

The expert commentary creates a political cue that nudges the government toward liberalising trade. That policy shift forces a reduction in tariff rates and pushes India to take a leading stance in WTO negotiations, which typically takes a few months. The reduced barriers immediately lower the cost of imported inputs and expand market access for exporters, a lag of weeks to months. Those cost changes then appear in corporate earnings and consumer price indices in the subsequent quarters.

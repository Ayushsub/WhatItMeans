Nifty reclaims 25,000 in best weekly rally in six years

Nifty's 8.58% weekly surge lifted index benchmarks while smaller stocks fell behind.

  • Nifty surged 8.58% over one week, reclaiming the 25,000 mark into Samvat 2083.
  • Heavyweight large-cap stocks drove headline gains, leaving smaller-cap equity portfolios trailing behind.
  • Whether foreign inflows continue pushing Nifty beyond the 25,000–25,200 resistance boundary.

What happened

During the week ending November 6, 2026, the Nifty 50 index surged 8.58% to close at 25,030, while the BSE Sensex rose 2.17% in a single session to 81,028. Reliance Industries recorded a 13% weekly gain, leading the benchmarks to their strongest weekly performance in nearly six years. The sharp rise was supported by foreign investor inflows, lower global crude oil prices, and favorable US midterm election outcomes.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over weeks

  1. step 1: Easing crude prices and US election results improve global investor sentiment
  2. step 2: Foreign institutional investors channel capital into liquid Indian large-cap stocks
  3. step 3: Index heavyweights experience rapid price gains, pulling up headline benchmark indices
  4. step 4: Nifty reclaims the 25,000 level even as mid- and small-cap stocks trail

Lower crude prices reduce India's import bill, strengthening the rupee and encouraging foreign institutional investors to enter the market. Capital inflows focus primarily on highly liquid, large-cap stocks such as Reliance Industries, causing them to jump sharply over a few trading sessions. Because benchmark indices like Nifty are weighted by market capitalization, these gains lift the broader index above key thresholds like 25,000. However, this liquidity does not immediately reach smaller companies, creating a gap between index performance and mid-cap portfolios.

Market reaction

The Sensex rose 1,718 points (2.17%) to 81,028 while the Nifty gained 493.75 points (2.01%) to settle at 25,030 on November 6, 2026. Over the week of November 2–6, the Nifty surged 8.58% and Reliance Industries jumped 13%.

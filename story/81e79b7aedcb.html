Tata Motors and Stellantis partner to manufacture export vehicles for 50 markets

Tata Motors will target exports across 50 international markets through a strategic architecture-sharing deal.

What happened

On October 17, 2026, Tata Motors announced an expanded partnership with global automaker Stellantis to jointly develop vehicles in India. Under the deal, Tata Motors will share its vehicle architecture and advanced technology with its partner. The alliance plans to export the resulting co-developed vehicles to more than 50 international markets.

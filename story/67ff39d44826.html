Renault targets 50% hybrid and CNG sales mix to lift India market share

Sub-1% market share pushes Renault toward ₹16.99 lakh strong hybrids.

  • Renault targets 50% hybrid and CNG sales mix within three years.
  • The move challenges incumbents like Maruti Suzuki in the fuel-efficient SUV segment.
  • Customer adoption of ₹16.99 lakh Duster hybrid will determine volume growth.
AutomobilesAuto Components

What happened

On October 17, 2026, Renault India CEO Stéphane Deblaise announced that strong hybrids and CNG models are projected to comprise 50% of the company's domestic sales mix within 2 to 3 years. The strategy includes launching the Renault Duster Strong Hybrid priced at ₹16.99 lakh, delivering an advertised 29 km/l mileage. Renault aims to lift its Indian market share from below 1% to between 3% and 5% by 2030 through localised powertrain options.

Jargon, in plain words

strong hybrid
A vehicle pairing a petrol engine with an electric motor and battery that can propel the car purely on electric power at low speeds without plugging in.
powertrain
The entire mechanism that generates power and delivers it to the road surface, including the engine, transmission, and drive shafts.

Nasdaq rebounds 1.73% after $2.5 trillion wiped out in AI selloff

A $2.5 trillion tech wipeout tests the heavy debt loads behind AI data centres.

What happened

On November 6, 2026, the Nasdaq bounced back 1.73%, led by semiconductor makers Nvidia and AMD following a steep drop that erased up to $2.5 trillion from the index. The initial selloff was spurred by US midterm election results giving Democrats control of Congress, stirring investor concerns over increased regulation on data-centre projects and AI infrastructure spending. At the same time, stronger-than-expected October US jobs data revived worries that the Federal Reserve might raise interest rates in December.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. Democratic victories in the US midterms spark expectations of stricter oversight on AI data centres
  2. Investors reassess the heavy corporate debt required to finance rapid computing infrastructure expansion
  3. Cloud and software giants face higher borrowing yields and slower regulatory clearances, threatening capital spending
  4. Chipmakers face potential slowdowns in high-margin hardware orders, hitting projected future revenue

The chain was triggered immediately when election returns raised the threat of stricter data-centre permitting and power regulation within days. Over the coming weeks, this political friction intersects with a bond market already strained by heavy corporate debt issuance, raising yields on infrastructure loans. Over several months, higher financing costs and regulatory hurdles could force cloud hyperscalers to pace their capital expenditure. That slowed deployment would eventually show up across subsequent quarters as softening sales and margin compression for chip suppliers.

Market reaction

The Nasdaq rose 1.73% on Friday led by Nvidia, AMD, Intel, and Micron, though it still completed its fourth consecutive weekly drop. The S&P 500 rose while the Dow Jones slipped, European equities declined about 1%, and Indian equities bucked global market weakness.

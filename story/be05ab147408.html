GST council imposes 5% tax on major platform operators

5% GST applies to rides, food and e‑commerce platforms alike

  • Uniform 5% GST announced for major platforms
  • Higher tax could raise prices for users

online platform servicesFiscal & Budget

What happened

On 9 October 2026 the GST Council endorsed a uniform 5% goods and services tax (GST) on major platform operators including Ola, Uber, Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon and Flipkart, irrespective of their business model. The decision was disclosed by CBIC member for GST Sanjay Mangal in a Mint report.

The council's decision forces platforms to pay an extra 5% tax on each transaction. This raises their cost base, which either squeezes profit margins or is shifted onto customers through higher fees. The price change appears to consumers within weeks, influencing their usage frequency and overall sector revenue.

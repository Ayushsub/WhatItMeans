KEC International wins ₹2,872 crore transmission line orders from PGCIL

A ₹2,872 crore order boost pushes KEC's year-to-date inflows past the ₹13,500 crore mark.

  • KEC International secured ₹2,872 crore power transmission contracts from PGCIL.
  • The deal expands year-to-date order intake past ₹13,500 crore, ensuring revenue visibility.
  • Execution speed and margin protection amidst fluctuating raw material costs will determine profitability.
Capital Goods & Engineering

What happened

On October 24, 2026, KEC International announced new orders worth ₹2,872 crore from Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd (PGCIL). The contracts cover the design, supply, and installation of 765 kV high-voltage transmission lines. With these contracts, KEC's year-to-date order intake reached over ₹13,500 crore, led by ₹9,500 crore in Transmission and Distribution (T&D) business wins.

Sitharaman projects over 10% economic growth driven by technology and startups

India's path to a 10% growth target relies heavily on its startup ecosystem.

  • Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman stated India can achieve over 10% economic growth.
  • Reaching this target requires deep technological integration and scaling up domestic startups.
  • Policy support for artificial intelligence and funding trends in the startup sector.
IT & TechStartups & Venture CapitalGrowth

What happened

On September 26, 2026, Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman stated that India has the potential to achieve an economic growth rate surpassing 10%. She emphasized that reaching this target requires scaling up multiple sectors through technological innovation and pushed back against claims that India has missed the artificial intelligence wave. Sitharaman highlighted the rapid growth of the domestic startup ecosystem since 2015 as evidence of this economic momentum.

The transmission mechanism

Plays out over quarters

  1. Government prioritizes technology and startup growth to hit the 10% target
  2. Policy incentives and digital public infrastructure expand to support tech adoption
  3. Traditional sectors integrate artificial intelligence and digital tools to improve productivity
  4. Higher productivity lowers operating costs and boosts corporate earnings across industries

The transmission begins with policy directives that encourage technology adoption, which typically takes several quarters to translate into actual corporate capital expenditure. As companies invest in artificial intelligence and digital tools, there is a lag of 12 to 18 months before these technologies are fully integrated into business workflows. Eventually, this integration drives up productivity, allowing firms to produce more output with fewer resources. This efficiency gain ultimately shows up as improved operating margins in corporate earnings reports.
